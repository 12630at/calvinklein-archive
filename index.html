timeline
1968

Calvin Klein founds his namesake company in New York with childhood friend Barry Schwartz. The first collection is a line of women's coats and suits.

1973

Klein wins the Coty American Fashion Critics' Award — the youngest designer ever to receive it. The first of three consecutive wins.

1976

The launch of Calvin Klein jeans. A new American aesthetic begins to take shape — clean lines, minimal cuts, raw denim.

1980

A fifteen-year-old Brooke Shields appears in a television campaign whispering: "Nothing comes between me and my Calvins." Provocation enters fashion advertising.

1982

The men's underwear line debuts. Bruce Weber photographs the Olympic pole vaulter Tom Hintnaus on a Santorini rooftop — the image redefines masculinity in advertising.

1985

Obsession is launched. The fragrance, with its languorous Bruce Weber campaigns, becomes a cultural artifact of the decade.

1988

Eternity arrives. A counterpoint to Obsession — softer, romantic, domestic. Klein understands that contradiction is part of desire.

1992

Mark Wahlberg and Kate Moss appear together in the underwear campaign shot by Herb Ritts. The black-and-white image becomes one of the most reproduced fashion photographs of the era.

1994

CK One launches — the first major unisex fragrance. Cast by Steven Meisel, the campaign is the visual blueprint of a generation.

1996

CK Be follows. The brand's reach into youth culture is now total — music, magazines, MTV, the bathroom shelves of every teenager in the western world.

2003

Calvin Klein steps back. Francisco Costa is appointed Creative Director of Women's, Italo Zucchelli of Men's. A new minimal language begins.

2008

Forty years of Calvin Klein. The archive is now a record of how a single brand mirrored — and shaped — American visual culture.

2016

Raf Simons is appointed Chief Creative Officer. He merges all divisions under a single vision — American iconography refracted through European precision.

2018

205W39NYC. Andy Warhol prints, prairie dresses, plastic firefighter coats. A spring/summer collection that reads American history as cinema.

2020

The modern era. The brand returns to its essence: bodies, fabric, light. The archive continues to write itself.
